# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single individual professional who is actively studying toward one or more
certifications (e.g. cloud, security, PM, healthcare). They use the app during
focused study sessions — often at a desk, sometimes at night, frequently over
long stretches. They are motivated but time-pressed, juggling exam dates,
study material scattered across PDFs/links/notes, and a sense of "am I on track?"
They reach for the keyboard before the mouse and expect the tool to keep up.

## Product Purpose

A personal home base for certification study. It exists to replace the
spreadsheet-plus-scattered-bookmarks status quo with one calm place that:

- **Centralizes** everything about each certification the user is pursuing —
  study materials, links, notes, exam details — so nothing lives in ten tabs.
- **Tracks study progress and prep** toward each cert, so the user always knows
  where they stand and what to do next.

Success looks like: the user opens the app and immediately knows what to study
today, feels a genuine (not manufactured) sense of momentum, and never loses a
study resource again.

## Brand Personality

**Warm, encouraging, quietly confident.** Studying for a certification is hard
and often lonely; the app is on the user's side. The voice is human and
supportive without being saccharine — it encourages by showing real progress,
not by cheering.

Three words: **supportive, focused, crisp.**

Emotional goals: calm reassurance ("you've got this, and here's proof"),
earned momentum, and zero friction. Warmth comes from tone, typography, and
generous space — never from clutter or noise.

## Anti-references

- **Corporate LMS / compliance dashboards.** No cluttered data tables, dated
  stock photography, clip-art badges, or enterprise-training drabness.
- **Cheesy gamification.** No confetti storms, cartoon mascots, XP-bar
  theatrics, or mobile-game badge walls. Progress should feel grown-up and
  earned.
- Not cold or sterile either — restrained is the execution, warm is the feeling.

## Design Principles

1. **Show progress, don't perform it.** Momentum is communicated through honest,
   legible progress — real completion, real dates — not celebratory noise.
   Encouragement is a byproduct of clarity.
2. **Calm under focus.** The interface stays quiet so the user's attention goes
   to studying, not to the UI. Restraint (Linear-like) is the default; warmth is
   layered in through tone and space, not decoration.
3. **One home for everything.** Every study material, link, and detail for a cert
   has an obvious place to live and an obvious way back to it. Nothing should
   tempt the user back to scattered tabs.
4. **Respect the keyboard.** Core flows are fast and keyboard-drivable; the mouse
   is optional, not required.
5. **Warm, not cute.** Human, supportive copy and a friendly palette — held to a
   grown-up standard. When a choice risks reading as gimmicky, choose the calmer
   option.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Target: WCAG 2.1 AA.** Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥ 3:1,
  visible focus states on every interactive element, full keyboard operability.
- **Dark mode is first-class,** not an afterthought — the user studies at night
  and for long sessions. Both light and dark themes must meet contrast targets.
- **Reduced motion** honored throughout (`prefers-reduced-motion`): every
  animation has a crossfade or instant alternative.
- **Status never by color alone** — expiring / in-progress / passed states carry
  an icon or label as well as color.
